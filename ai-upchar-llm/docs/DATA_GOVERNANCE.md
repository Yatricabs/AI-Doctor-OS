# Data governance

Allowed inputs require provenance, usage rights, retrieval date, language,
domain, and quality approval. Arbitrary scraped pages are rejected. Copyrighted
content is included only when its license or written authorization permits the
intended training use.

Patient interactions are quarantined by default. Required gates are privacy
screening, de-identification, eligibility review, quality review, abstraction,
and explicit export approval. Names, phones, email, identifiers, exact addresses,
and raw conversations are forbidden in training exports. Rejections and counts
are logged; unnecessary raw conversation logging is prohibited.
